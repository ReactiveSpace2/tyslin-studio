# Code of conduct

Tyslin Studio is built by people who want AI agents doing real work. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else the project meets, is expected to keep it a good place to work.

## What we expect

- Be direct and kind. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Review the work, not the author. Say what should change and why.
- Welcome newcomers. A first pull request takes courage; make it a good experience.
- Respect people's time: keep issues on topic and pull requests focused.

## What we don't accept

- Insults, harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Spam, advertising or repeatedly reopening settled discussions.

## Reporting

If something happens that breaks these rules, write to **info@tyslin.ai**. Reports are read by the maintainers at ReactiveSpace, kept confidential, and answered within a week. Depending on what happened, the response ranges from a private word to removal from the project.

Maintainers who break these rules are held to the same standard.
